Frequently Asked Questions

How is pricing calculated?

Pricing depends on property size, current condition, service type, and frequency. We provide a free quote before work begins.

What is included in a standard cleaning?

Standard cleans typically include kitchens, bathrooms, dusting, floors, trash removal, and high-touch surfaces.

Do you bring supplies?

Yes. Our teams arrive with professional supplies and equipment, and we can use your preferred products on request.

How far ahead should I schedule?

Most clients book 3–7 days ahead, but same-week openings are often available depending on your area.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please give at least 24 hours of notice so we can offer the appointment to another customer.

What areas do you serve?

Cleaning US serves residential and commercial clients across local metro neighborhoods and nearby suburbs.
